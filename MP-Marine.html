FAQ

Everything you need to know before ordering a handmade ship model.

What scales do you work in?

We commonly work in scales from 1:200 to 1:50, depending on the vessel and intended display size.

How long does a custom model take?

Lead times vary by complexity and size, typically ranging from 6 to 14 weeks from design approval.

Can you replicate a specific ship?

Yes. Share photos, plans, or references, and we’ll design a model that matches your chosen vessel.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship worldwide with careful packaging and insured carriers to protect delicate models.

How do I start a commission?

Use the inquiry form below to describe your idea. We’ll respond with options, timelines, and pricing.

Thinking about a model?

Tell us about the ship you have in mind. We’ll get back with details and next steps.
